## Licence notes — read before a public release

* **NPPA** (`nlem2022`, `nppa2022`). nppa.gov.in copyright policy: material
  "may be reproduced free of charge in any format or media without requiring
  specific permission", provided it is reproduced accurately, not used in a
  misleading context, and the source is prominently acknowledged. This file
  is that acknowledgement; the app's About screen should carry it too.
* **NLEM via CDSCO is deliberately not used.** CDSCO also publishes the NLEM
  2022 PDF, but cdsco.gov.in's copyright policy says its contents "may not be
  reproduced … without due permission". The same list is taken from the
  Gazette notification hosted by NPPA instead.
* **Jan Aushadhi** (`janaushadhi`). The janaushadhi.gov.in portal's copyright
  notice says its material may be reproduced free of charge *after taking
  permission by mail*, accurately, with the source acknowledged. **That
  permission has not been requested yet.** The data used is factual (generic
  names and strengths of products the scheme sells), but until PMBI agrees,
  treat it as not cleared for distribution: either email PMBI for permission,
  or rebuild without it —
  `python tools/catalog/build_catalog.py --without-janaushadhi` (gives 51
  brands + 370 generics, 64 KB; ALLEGRA's fexofenadine and URIMAX's
  tamsulosin are then not in any government list).
* **Curated brands** are written by this project from general knowledge, not
  copied from any dataset. Rules are at the top of `curated_brands.json`:
  only certain facts, only strengths known to be sold, combination contents
  only when certain. Each entry is marked `"source": "curated"`.
* **Not used, on purpose:** 1mg, PharmEasy, Netmeds, Medindia or any Kaggle /
  GitHub scrape of them. Their licence is unclear at best.
* `lookalikes.json` is curated by this project (see its `about` field).

## How the lists become entries

* One **generic** entry per set of salts (and per release type for a single
  salt: `METFORMIN`, `METFORMIN SR`). Pharmacopoeia marks and salt forms are
  dropped (`Metformin Hydrochloride IP` → `metformin`); a handful of spellings
  are folded (`acetylsalicylic acid` → `aspirin`, `thyroxine` →
  `levothyroxine`, `frusemide` → `furosemide`).
* The Indian Pharmacopoeia's "prolonged-release" is filed under `SR`, the label
  Indian prescriptions use for it.
* Strengths are recorded for tablets and capsules only, and for a combination
  only when every salt's amount is printed. Liquids, creams and injections
  keep their form with no strengths, so they never raise "unusual strength".
* Rows the parser cannot read cleanly are skipped, never guessed. Last build:
  Jan Aushadhi 1,984 read / 92 skipped (surgical goods and nutraceuticals
  excluded before that), NLEM 846 / 45, NPPA 1,004 / 64. Sterile water is
  dropped (a diluent, not a medicine), and spellings of one salt that differ
  only in spaces or hyphens are folded into one.
