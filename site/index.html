How it works

You never have to find the button.

Winger listens for trouble (a scream, "help me", a missed dose, a stop that runs too long) and climbs a ladder one rung at a time. Answer at any rung and it stands down.

Rung 1

Nudge

A quiet vibration. Most of the time, that's all it takes.

Rung 2

Ask

"Are you okay?" A full-screen check-in, spoken out loud.

Rung 3

Guardian

An SMS with your location and a live link goes to everyone you chose, and Winger calls the first.

Rung 4

112

A countdown to the emergency dialler. Only the safety preset can ever reach this rung.

Two PINs. Your PIN stops everything and tells guardians you're safe. The duress PIN looks exactly the same, but keeps alerting in secret.

A call from Riya

A real-looking phone call with an ElevenLabs voice agent. Say your safe phrase mid-call and guardians are alerted while the call carries on.

Guardians need no app

A link opens a live map, status and timeline in any browser. If the phone goes dark, the page says so within a few minutes.

Works offline

SMS, calls, safe phrases and an offline companion run on the phone. The cloud and the Hub are extras, never requirements.

Tamper-evident locker

Evidence is encrypted on the phone in 5-second chunks chained by hash. Any edit shows up.

Set a route

Winger stays quiet on the way, and speaks up if you leave the route, stop too long or run late.

Help when it's not an emergency

Step-by-step guides for stalking, blackmail, morphed photos or a refused FIR, with the right helpline one tap away.

One engine, three jobs

Presets point Winger at what matters to you.

Every preset uses the same engine: watch, check in, then bring in people. Safety always comes first. A dose reminder never delays or hides a safety alert.

Winger Hub

Turn a spare laptop into your household's server.

Winger Hub is a small desktop app. Run it on any computer at home and it takes over the cloud's job: live links for guardians, the dead-man switch, and pairing for your phones. Your data stays in your house.

  • Drop-in for the cloudThe Hub speaks the same API as Winger's cloud backend, so the phone app works with either one.
  • Guardian links served from homeThe live map opens on your Hub. Add a free Tailscale Funnel address to reach guardians outside your Wi-Fi.
  • Dead-man switchIf a phone stops checking in for 3 minutes during a session, its guardians' page turns red.
  • Private by designThe Hub's screen shows counts, never names, numbers or locations. Phones pair with a one-time code, and only token hashes are stored.
  • The phone never depends on itIf the Hub is off, every alert still goes out from the phone.

What we promise

Built for the person being watched over.

The phone works alone

Every alert has an on-phone path. No internet, no Hub, no problem.

Her data is hers

Safety sessions are visible only to the guardians she picks, even inside her own household.

No secrets shipped

PINs are stored only as salted hashes. No API keys live in the app, the repo or the Hub.

Honest words

"Helps you get help faster", never "keeps you safe". Medicine reminders are not medical advice.
